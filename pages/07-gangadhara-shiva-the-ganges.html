ELEPHANTA GUIDE
STOP 07

Gangadhara — Shiva & the Ganges

Now find Gangadhara.

This is the descent of the Ganges.

The story is one of the great pieces of Indian mythology, and it gives us another spectacular image of controlled power.

Bhagiratha, the legendary king, performs severe austerities to bring the celestial Ganga to Earth.

The river's descent is too powerful to be allowed to strike the Earth directly.

Shiva therefore receives it in his matted hair.

The river is broken and controlled through Shiva's intervention.

Look at the composition.

The scene contains many figures.

It is crowded.

It is dramatic.

The sculptors were not trying to create a quiet portrait.

They were creating an event.

Now look at Shiva.

He is the stabilizing presence.

Everything else is movement.

That is the trick.

The scene is full of activity, but Shiva controls it.

Again and again, Elephanta returns to controlled power.

This is one of the cave's central ideas.

Now think about the story as a metaphor.

A force of enormous potential can destroy if uncontrolled.

The same force can nourish life when properly directed.

The Ganges is both dangerous and life-giving.

Shiva does not destroy it.

He controls it.

That is a much more interesting idea than simply “Shiva catches a river.”
